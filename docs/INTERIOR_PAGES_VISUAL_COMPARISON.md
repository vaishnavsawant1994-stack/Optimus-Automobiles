# Interior Pages Visual Comparison

The five supplied reference images were used as the visual direction for the implementation.

## Matched Characteristics

- Cinematic black page background and restrained gold accents
- Compact utility bar and navigation shell
- Wide image-led interior-page heroes
- Serif display headings and dense sans-serif interface text
- Inventory filter/sidebar proportions and four-column desktop grid
- Horizontal selling and ownership process layouts
- Split service, showroom and founder content bands
- Three-column contact workspace
- Compact multi-column footer

## Responsive Transformations

- Inventory filters move to an accessible drawer below 900px.
- Vehicle results change from four to three, two and one columns.
- Forms use one field per row on mobile.
- Feature grids and trust strips reduce to two columns.
- Split image/content sections stack without changing reading order.
- Contact actions become a safe-area-aware sticky mobile bar.
